# Complete candidate classification of metaplectic time reversal

Polaris, 14 November 2026, working proof for independent checking.
NOT a preprint; no independently checked new theorem yet.

The intended main result comprises the full semilinear braided
autoequivalence 2-group, all unmarked coherent C2^T actions, their arithmetic
existence and orbit counts, and both anomaly signs. The crossed TY model,
ordinary TY autoequivalence classification, equivariantisation theorem,
odd-primary quadratic-form classification and anomaly formulas are PRIOR.

## 1. Setup and candidate theorem

Let (A,Q) be a nontrivial finite nondegenerate metric group of odd order N.
Here Q:A->U(1) is homogeneous, Q(na)=Q(a)^(n^2). Let nu in {+1,-1}.
Use the unitary modular category

    M=M(A,Q,nu) = TY(A,B_q,nu | q,alpha,nu/alpha)//C2,
    q=Q^(1/2), alpha^2=nu G(q^-1), G(f)=N^(-1/2) sum_a f(a),

in Galindo--Lentner--Moeller [GLM, section 3] conventions. The square root
q is the unique homogeneous quadratic form with odd-order values and
q^2=Q. The generator g of C2 acts strictly by inversion on A and fixes
the TY noninvertible object m. If Q admits an anti-isometry, then
G(q^-1) is real, and put d=nu G(q^-1)=(-1)^epsilon.

THEOREM CANDIDATE.

(a) If Q is not isometric to Q^-1 there are no antilinear braided
autoequivalences of M. Otherwise its full dagger-preserving semilinear
braided autoequivalence 2-group is represented by the crossed module

    partial:E=C2_u x C2_z -> O^pm(A,Q),
    partial(u)=-I, partial(z)=I,
    h.u=u z^(epsilon w(h)), h.z=z.

O^pm is the group of isometries and anti-isometries and w their parity.
Its Postnikov class is epsilon*w cup c, where c in H2(O^pm/{+/-I},C2)
is the central inversion-cover class. If Q has no anti-isometry, the
unitary part has the same model with O(A,Q) and trivial source action.

(b) A coherent C2^T action is represented by (r,s), where r is an
anti-isometry, s in {+1,-1}, and either

    r^2=I, or r^2=-I and d=1.

All equivalences allowing unitary braided relabelling are generated by

    (r,s) ~ (h r h^-1,s) for h in O(A,Q),
    (r,s) ~ (-r,d s).

For r^2=I the canonical boson b has Kramers sign +1; for r^2=-I it has
Kramers sign -1. At a FIXED homomorphism into pi_0 of the symmetry
2-group, coherent structures before unmarked conjugation form a C2
torsor. The nonzero localization obstruction occurs exactly for r^2=-I,
d=-1, and is represented by the odd grading sign z.

(c) Write the odd-primary quadratic Jordan data as (n_(p,k),D_(p,k)),
where n is the rank over Z/p^k and D the unit determinant square class of
the coefficient matrix in the additive quadratic form x^t M x/p^k.
Then Q is self-reverse if and only if n_(p,k) is even for every p=3 mod4.
Every self-reverse Q admits an anti-isometry squaring to -I. An
anti-isometry squaring to I exists exactly when Q is hyperbolic:

    (A,Q) = H(B) = (B directsum B^vee, Q(x,chi)=chi(x)).

Equivalently every n_(p,k) is even and
D_(p,k)=(-1)^(n_(p,k)/2) modulo squares. Hyperbolicity is stronger than
metabolicity. Therefore a coherent C2^T action exists exactly when Q
is self-reverse and (d=1 or Q is hyperbolic).

(d) For a self-reverse Q let S={(p,k):p=1 mod4,n_(p,k)>0},

    P = product_(p,k in S) (2 n_(p,k)),
    F = 0 if some (p,k) in S has n odd or D nonsquare,
        2^|S| otherwise.

Empty products are 1. The number of UNMARKED coherent C2^T action classes is

    d=1:  P+F + 2*[Q hyperbolic],
    d=-1: [Q hyperbolic].

More informative labels are: for r^2=I a single O-conjugacy class, with
two fractionalizations for d=1 and one unmarked class for d=-1; for
r^2=-I the O-conjugacy invariants are the signed metric eigenspace data
at each split prime p=1 mod4, modulo simultaneous exchange of all signs.
At nonsplit primes p=3 mod4 there is a single O-conjugacy class.

(e) The full bosonic time-reversal anomaly, in coordinates
(Z(CP2),Z(RP4)), is

    r^2=I:  (+1,+1),
    r^2=-I: (G(Q),s),

where in the second line choose m_+ as the unique defect simple of twist
+1, and normalize s to its Kramers sign. The two fractionalizations of
each r^2=-I permutation have opposite degree-four anomaly. The first
coordinate is the gravitational anomaly. These coordinates classify the
invertible unoriented four-dimensional anomaly using standard prior
unoriented bordism/anomaly theory, not a new indicator-completeness theorem.

## 2. Explicit crossed model and semilinear functors

The nontrivial associators of D=TY(A,B_q,nu) are the bicharacter scalars
and the nu/sqrt(N) bicharacter Fourier matrix. The crossed braiding is

    c_ab=B_q(a,b), c_am=c_ma=q(a)^-1, c_mm|_a=alpha q(a).

The positive ribbon scalar on m is beta=nu/alpha. Every isometry or
anti-isometry h acts by relabelling a->ha, m->m, with canonical relabelling
of direct-sum summands as tensorator; in the anti case it conjugates all
morphism scalars. The associator equations follow termwise, including the
Fourier matrix. Composition of these functors is strict.

For a linear h the crossed equivariance constraint F_h g -> g F_h is
the identity. For an antilinear h it must have components 1 on a and d
on m, since the m,m braiding square is bar(alpha)=d alpha; all other
braiding squares use q(ha)=bar(q(a)). This constraint is a monoidal
natural transformation and its square is the identity. Ribbon compatibility
uses bar(beta)=d beta. All coefficients and relabellings are unitary.

Thus on equivariant objects (V,v:gV->V) the induced functor is

    T_h(V,v)=(F_h V, F_h(v) psi_V^-1).

The same formula works for linear h with psi=1. Since d is real, these
give a strict group of functors T_h for h in O^pm.

## 3. Exhaustion of functors and natural transformations

M has precisely two invertible simples 1,b; b is a boson. Since N>1 and
N is odd, the other simples have dimensions 2 and sqrt(N)>1. Every
dagger-preserving braided equivalence preserves b and hence the canonical
Rep(C2) embedding up to tensor isomorphism. Individually its restriction
can be identified with the identity (or scalar conjugation) in exactly two
ways, differing by the sign on b. This follows by solving the single
tensor constraint on b*b=1: a nonzero complex scalar has two square roots.

DGNO Theorem 4.44 is a 2-equivalence for braided categories containing a
specified Rep(G). Therefore after choosing one such identification, the
functor lifts to a crossed equivalence of D. Antilinear functors are
linear functors from the conjugate category, so the theorem applies.
There is no assumption that the WHOLE C2^T action preserves the boson
algebra equivariantly.

By Edie-Michell Lemma 2.16/Remark 2.17, every tensor equivalence of TY is
monoidally isomorphic to strict F_h for a unique bicharacter isometry.
In the antilinear case the same proof gives an anti-isometry, or one can
compose with one explicitly constructed semilinear F_h. No additional
object-fixing tensor functors survive monoidal isomorphism. After this
normalization, all commutation constraints differ by Aut_tensor(Id_D)=C2,
whose nontrivial element is -1 on m and +1 on A. The crossed braiding
forces the above unique value 1 or d. Hence all functors are T_h.

Changing the chosen restriction identification on Rep(C2) is the deck
inversion of de-equivariantisation, so changes h to -h. One can also see
this directly in DGNO's module model: the two isomorphisms of the regular
algebra differ by its nontrivial right translation, inducing g.

An isomorphism between T_h and T_k with restriction +1 on b lifts under
the 2-equivalence to an isomorphism F_h->F_k; this requires h=k. Its two
possibilities are Id and z, by the TY natural-automorphism computation.
An isomorphism with restriction -1 on b is reduced to that case by
composing with the canonical equivariance transformation

    U_(V,v)=v:T_g(V,v)->(V,v).

It exists precisely when k=-h and has the two possibilities U,zU.
Notice U is NOT a morphism over the specified Rep(C2), since U_b=-1.
This explains why the inversion quotient appears only after forgetting
the chosen restriction identification. No other morphisms can occur.

Horizontal composition satisfies U^2=1, z^2=1 and Uz=zU, directly from
v*g(v)=1. Linear T_h commutes with U; antilinear T_h conjugates U to
U z^epsilon, by comparing the new equivariance map F_h(v)psi^-1 with
F_h(v). Thus the groupoid of actual functors and transformations is
exactly the crossed module in (a). Its Peiffer relations hold since
inversion is central and has even parity.

Choose a section of O^pm/{+/-I} into O^pm, with central factor set c.
The compositors U^c have associativity defect z^(epsilon w cup c).
This proves the Postnikov formula. It also gives the corresponding
obstruction law for any finite symmetry group mapping into pi_0, although
the classification here specializes to C2^T.

## 4. C2 actions, fractionalization and affine conjugation

An antilinear class represented by r is involutive iff r^2 a=+/-a for
every a. The group A is the union of ker(r^2-I) and ker(r^2+I), so one
kernel is all of A: a group cannot be a union of two proper subgroups.
Thus r^2=I or -I GLOBALLY; the sign cannot vary between primary blocks.

When r^2=I, T_r^2=Id and a square trivialization is eta=z^k. When
r^2=-I, T_r^2=T_g and eta=z^k U. The C2 pentagon for eta is automatic
in the first case; in the second it fails by z^epsilon as computed in
section 3. Since Aut_tensor(Id_M)=C2 and T fixes z, no change in eta
removes this defect. Its cohomology class is the generator of
H3(C2,C2) if epsilon=1. When it vanishes, both k give coherent actions.
No natural automorphism of T_r changes k, since its norm is z^(2k)=1.

Here Aut_tensor(Id_M)=C2 also follows directly from fusion: the fusion
X_a*m_+ = m_+ + m_- forces all even values to 1 and both odd values
equal; m_+^2 contains 1 and forces the common odd value to +/-1.

Unitary conjugation T_h T_r T_h^-1=T_(h r h^-1) preserves z and U,
and hence k. Replacing r by -r uses the natural transformation U on
T_r(V). On an odd simple m_e with equivariance eigenvalue e=+/-1,
this transformation has scalar d e. Its norm under T_r has scalar d.
Therefore changing representative sends s=(-1)^k to d s. These generate
all action equivalences because section 3 exhausted all functors and
all natural isomorphisms. This proves (b), including unmarked equivalence.

The square trivialization on b is +1 for r^2=I and U_b=-1 for r^2=-I.
This retains both boson fractionalizations without demanding an
equivariant structure on 1+b.

## 5. Odd-primary arithmetic and all conjugacy classes

We use the standard rank/determinant classification of nonsingular
symmetric finite linking forms over an odd-residue DVR; in the Z_p case
this is exactly odd-primary quadratic Jordan classification. For
completeness the same argument works over a finite unramified extension:
pick a cyclic summand of maximal order with unit diagonal pairing,
split its orthogonal complement, and iterate. To justify the pivot,
nondegeneracy gives maximal-order x,y with pairing of maximal denominator;
one of x,y,x+y has diagonal pairing of maximal denominator, since 2 is
invertible. Projection using that diagonal entry splits the cyclic
summand. Equal-scale unimodular forms are classified by rank and unit
determinant square class over the finite residue field. Any residue-field
isometry lifts: if its Gram-matrix error at one stage is p^j E with E
symmetric, correct it by I+p^j S, with S=-M^(-1)E/2. Induction gives an
isometry over every quotient of the DVR.

For a finite module L the scale invariants are intrinsic: on

    Delta_k(L)=L[p^k]/(L[p^(k-1)]+p L[p^(k+1)])

the pairing p^(k-1) beta induces a nondegenerate residue-field form.
Both denominator summands pair to zero under this rescaling. After any
orthogonal cyclic splitting only the order-p^k summands contribute to
Delta_k. Thus its rank and determinant recover precisely the equal-scale
data independently of the splitting. Taking beta/2 instead tracks the
quadratic coefficient-matrix convention used in the theorem. This proves
uniqueness and shows that mixing Jordan levels creates no additional
isometry invariants. In particular we do NOT assume that a chosen Z_p
Jordan decomposition is stable under the extra operator.

Self-reversal multiplies the determinant at each scale by (-1)^n. This
preserves its square class automatically at p=1 mod4 and exactly for
even n at p=3 mod4. This proves the self-reversal criterion.

Let J^2=-I be an anti-isometry. Its additive polar form B satisfies
B(Jx,y)=B(x,Jy), since B(Jx,Jy)=-B(x,y). Treat the p-primary component
as a module over R_p=Z_p[t]/(t^2+1), t acting by J.

At p=1 mod4 this is the split etale algebra Z_p x Z_p. Its two
idempotents give the orthogonal +/-i_p eigenspaces. Hence conjugacy of
J is exactly isometry of this ORDERED pair of finite quadratic modules.
For each scale k, choose rank m of the positive eigenspace, rank n-m
of the negative one and their determinant classes D_+,D_- with product D.
There are 2n choices: one for each endpoint m=0,n and two for each
0<m<n. All choices occur by odd-primary classification.

At p=3 mod4 let K=Q_p(i), O=Z_p[i], the unramified quadratic extension.
Promote B to the symmetric O-bilinear linking form

    beta(x,y) = (B(x,y)-i B(Jx,y))/2 in K/O.

It is O-bilinear by self-adjointness and J^2=-I; Tr(beta)=B, and it is
nondegenerate since a vector orthogonal for beta is orthogonal for B.
Conversely the trace of a nonsingular O-bilinear form, with J=i, gives
such an anti-isometry. This is an equivalence of the relevant isometry
groupoids, including non-free modules and mixed scales.

At scale p^k let beta have rank m and unit determinant D_ext. Its trace
quadratic form over Z_p has rank 2m and determinant square class

    D_base = (-1)^m Norm_(O/Z_p)(D_ext).

Indeed on a rank-one O summand with coefficient a+bi, the additive
quadratic coefficient matrix of Tr(beta)/2 is [[a,-b],[-b,-a]], of
determinant -Norm(a+bi). Norm induces a bijection on unit square classes
of O and Z_p: both have two, and a residue-field generator maps to a
generator. Therefore every base quadratic constituent of even rank
has a unique extension-form class. This proves both existence and the
unique O(A_p,Q)-conjugacy class of J. Conjugation of coefficients preserves
their square classes, so an underlying isometry interchanges J and -J.

Thus every self-reverse Q admits J globally. The classification is at the
level of the etale module rather than an assumed invariant cyclic splitting.

For r^2=I an anti-isometry, the idempotents (I+/-r)/2 split A as A_++A_-.
The form is trivial on each eigenspace and pairs them perfectly. Hence
Q=H(A_+). Conversely the displayed hyperbolic form has such r. The
abstract type of A_+ is uniquely determined by half the multiplicities
of A, so all anti-involutions are conjugate. A symmetric identification
A_+~A_+^vee gives an isometry exchanging the two summands and sending
r to -r. Standard Jordan classification yields the determinant criterion
for hyperbolicity in (c).

To count J modulo sign, multiply the 2n choices at split primes; nonsplit
primes each contribute one. Negation simultaneously exchanges the two
metric eigenspaces in every split constituent. A fixed orbit requires
n even, m=n/2 and D_+=D_-, hence D square. When this holds there are two
choices at every split constituent. Therefore the fixed-point count is F
and Burnside gives (P+F)/2. For d=1, each such orbit retains two action
structures. The hyperbolic anti-involution contributes two if d=1; if
d=-1 its two structures are exchanged by the isometry sending r to -r.
This proves (c),(d), subject to the full exhaustion model in section 3.

## 6. Anomaly evaluation

The simples and twists are

    1,b: dimension 1, twist 1;
    X_[a], [a]={a,-a} nonzero: dimension 2, twist Q(a);
    m_e: dimension sqrt(N), twist e beta, e=+/-1.

The standard CP2 indicator is

    (2+4 sum_[a] Q(a)+N beta-N beta)/(2sqrt(N)) = G(Q).

For r^2=-I coherence forces d=1, so beta is real. No nonzero [a] is
fixed: ra=+/-a would give r^2a=a=-a and hence a=0. The boson has eta_b=-1,
so 1,b cancel in the RP4 sum. With eta=z^k U, eta_(m_e)=s e. Thus

    Z(RP4) = beta s.

Choose the square root alpha so beta=1, which is allowed by GLM Remark
3.4 and makes m_+ the twist +1 simple. Then Z(RP4)=s. The alternative
labelling gives the invariant expression theta_(m_+) eta_(m_+).

For r^2=I, Q is hyperbolic and G(Q)=G(q^-1)=1. If |A_+|=|A_-|=M0,
then N=M0^2. Fixed nonzero inversion orbits are exactly those contained
in A_+ or A_-; there are M0-1 of them in total. They all have twist 1
and eta_X=1. Along with 1,b their RP4 numerator is 2+2(M0-1)=2M0.
If d=-1 the m_e are exchanged and do not contribute. If d=1 they are
fixed, have opposite twists, and equal eta=s, so their contributions
cancel. Thus Z(RP4)=1 in either case, proving (e).

The use of the RP4 and CP2 values as complete coordinates of the bosonic
time-reversal anomaly is prior. The gluing derivation of the indicators
is in Barkeshli--Bonderson--Cheng--Jian--Walker arXiv:1612.07792 section
VII.B, equations (184),(191). Their state-sum construction and standard
unoriented bordism identify the anomaly with a character of Omega_4^O,
whose two generators may be taken as RP4 and CP2. The assertion here is
classification of invertible TQFT anomalies. Microscopic Hamiltonian
realizability is not an additional theorem asserted by this account.

## 7. Prescribed control and novelty boundary

A=(Z/9)^2, Q(x,y)=zeta_9^(x^2+y^2), J=[[2,2],[2,-2]]. Matrix identities
give J^2=J^tJ=-I. Irreducibility of t^2+1 mod 3 rules out a J-stable
cyclic order-9 summand. The Jordan rank is n_(3,2)=2 and determinant 1,
whereas hyperbolicity would require determinant -1, nonsquare mod3.
Thus Q is self-reverse and nonhyperbolic. Its Gauss sums G(Q) and
G(Q^-1/2) are 1: for every unit c mod9,

    sum_x zeta_9^(c x^2)=3+2(zeta_9^c+zeta_9^(4c)+zeta_9^(7c))=3.

The candidate classification gives no coherent C2^T action for nu=-1;
for nu=+1 it gives two unmarked actions, both eta_b=-1, with anomalies
(+1,+1) and (+1,-1). This is the consequence of the WHOLE theorem, not
a proposed isolated example paper.

## 8. Further prior boundaries and exact sources

BJLP's equivariant-condensation criterion applies to an ALREADY coherent
action and an invariant connected algebra. Here the individual functor
is lifted after choosing an algebra identification; the action coherence
on that algebra is allowed to fail. In particular an action with eta_b=-1
is not excluded by lack of an equivariant algebra structure on 1+b.

Plavnik--Sanford--Sconce classify real non-split TY fusion categories, and
Wen--Schaefer-Nameki discuss Galois-real TY categories with a noninvertible
defect declared antilinear. That changes the scalar action of the defect
inside the fusion category. Here TY is a fixed COMPLEX category with an
ordinary linear defect and FS sign nu; an EXTERNAL antilinear braided
autoequivalence acts on its equivariantisation. These are different
classification problems, not interchangeable constructions.
